How much does AC repair cost in Houston?

Most repairs land between $150 and $650. Capacitors and contactors sit at the low end, refrigerant leaks and fan motors at the high end. The $89 diagnostic is credited toward the repair if you go ahead with it.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If the system is over 12 years old, uses R-22 refrigerant, or the repair costs more than a third of a new unit, replacement usually makes more sense. Your tech shows you both numbers side by side and you decide.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. New systems can be financed with approved credit, including plans with 0% interest for a promotional period. Ask during the free estimate.

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

No. Emergency service costs the same at 2 a.m. on a Sunday as at 10 a.m. on a Tuesday.

Are you licensed and insured?

Every Texas HVAC contractor must hold a TDLR air conditioning and refrigeration license. Ours is printed on every invoice and at the bottom of this page, and we carry full liability insurance.
