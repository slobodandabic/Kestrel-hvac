Why it matters here

Built for Gulf Coast summers

An AC in Houston works harder than almost anywhere in the country. It has to fight the heat and the humidity, for months at a time, with the air handler sitting in a scorching attic. We install and repair with that in mind.

2×per year

Service twice, not once

A spring visit for cooling and a fall visit for heating. Houston systems can run most of the year, so an annual check isn't enough.

50%indoor RH

Target the humidity

An oversized unit cools the air fast and shuts off before it dries it. Correct sizing keeps indoor humidity under about 50%, which is where your home starts to feel comfortable.

Jun–Novstorm season

Hurricane-ready

Condenser tie-downs, surge protection and a post-storm inspection so you know your system is safe to turn back on after the power returns.

FAQ

Straight answers

Can't find your question? Call us. The person who answers knows HVAC, not a script.

How much does AC repair cost in Houston?

Most repairs land between $150 and $650. Capacitors and contactors sit at the low end; refrigerant leaks and fan motors at the high end. Our $89 diagnostic is credited toward the repair if you go ahead with it.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If your system is over 12 years old, uses R-22 refrigerant, or the repair costs more than a third of a new unit, replacement usually makes more sense. Your tech will show you both numbers side by side and let you decide.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. New systems can be financed with approved credit, including plans with 0% interest for a promotional period. Ask your comfort advisor during the free estimate.

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

No. Emergency service costs the same at 2 a.m. on a Sunday as it does at 10 a.m. on a Tuesday.

Are you licensed and insured?

Every Texas HVAC contractor must hold a TDLR air conditioning and refrigeration contractor license. Ours is printed on every invoice and at the bottom of this page, and we carry full liability insurance.
